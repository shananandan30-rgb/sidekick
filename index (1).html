Sidekick

A friendly helper for young caregivers in Singapore. Sidekick can help with:

  • Money and care services: grants like the Home Caregiving Grant, home care, insurance, and everyday needs (e.g. "money for nappies")
  • Planning your week around school, work and care tasks (you can send a timetable screenshot)
  • Someone to talk to when things feel heavy
  • Finding support groups and other young caregivers

Click the chat bubble in the bottom-right corner to start. You can type, tap the buttons, or use the mic.

Sidekick is a student prototype and not an official government service. Always check scheme details on aic.sg or call the AIC hotline 1800-650-6060. In an emergency call 995; for emotional crisis support call SOS 1767.
